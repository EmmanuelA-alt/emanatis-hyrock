Dear Mr. Loisi,

Thank you for providing your financing checklist. I have assembled the requested documentation and present my application for a 12-month bridging loan in excess of CHF 600,000, collateralized by my property in La Punt-Chamues-ch, Graubünden.

The Opportunity

I am seeking a 12-month bridging loan in excess of CHF 600,000 (final amount subject to Hyrock's assessment of the collateral), collateralized by a revenue-generating, appreciating residential property I own in La Punt-Chamues-ch, Graubünden — held free and clear as my sole property since 2016, acquired through inheritance.

The property is currently tenanted under a stable, long-term arrangement (10+ years) generating €2,000/month in net rental income. The municipal assessment values the property at CHF 760,000, with market comparables supporting a valuation of approximately CHF 1,100,000. Full details and source documentation are provided in this package.

Why a Swiss Bridge, and Why Now

Since the Greek financial crisis, Greek financial institutions have been slow to respond to entrepreneurial and SME liquidity demands. Regulatory approvals are difficult, fees are expensive, and typical lenders are restricted from extending credit to construction companies and borrowers with limited domestic credit history — which is my situation.

I believe a Swiss bridging facility, secured against a Swiss asset I own outright, is the right structure: fast, clean, and backed by tangible collateral in Hyrock's home jurisdiction.

What the Capital Enables

The loan proceeds will serve as a force multiplier for my business activities in Greece. I am the founder and principal shareholder of two operating companies:

  • Emanatis Luxury Living — a 10-unit LEED Platinum residential development in Galatsi, Athens. The project is approximately 60% complete with a current portfolio value of €3,820,140 at asking prices. Four units are sold with €1,319,000 in confirmed revenue on the books. The remaining six units are valued at €2,501,140. Construction is insured with Orizon 1964 (policy 2184000708). I hold a 99% equity position.
  • DreamLease S.A. — a Tesla-only vehicle leasing platform serving the Greek market, incorporated December 2025. We have just placed our first two vehicles with clients, with a third arriving early next month. The platform is operational and scaling. I hold a 90% equity position.

I seek only a short-term bridge because my revenue position is strong: units are selling at asking price with no negotiation, demand is robust, and completion is targeted for late 2026. A bridge facility provides the liquidity to maintain momentum without diluting equity or restructuring around Greek bank timelines.

My Financial Position

I am self-employed and currently draw no salary from either company. Any future compensation will be structured as dividends, taxed at 5% under Greek law. I have zero personal liabilities: no credit card debt, no personal loans, no mortgages, no outstanding tax obligations. My 2026 Greek ENFIA (Unified Property Tax) is fully settled — the clearance certificate from AADE is enclosed, translated for your review.

I have personally loaned the two companies a combined total of €1,000,000 in shareholder loans — €900,000 to Emanatis Luxury Living and €100,000 to DreamLease (which funded the first two vehicles and startup costs) — repayable within five years at approximately 5.5–6.5% annual interest, compounding. These loans may continue up to €5,000,000 as the business scales. They are not capital contributions and do not increase share value — they are arm's-length debt obligations of the companies to me as creditor.

In addition to the Swiss collateral, I hold a 99% equity position in Emanatis Luxury Living and a 90% equity position in DreamLease S.A., representing significant further asset backing.

Long-Term Relationship Vision

I view this bridge as the beginning of a recurring financing relationship. I intend to use the La Punt property as a recurring leverage unit for additional funding on a case-by-case, project-by-project basis as my portfolio grows. Each project cycle — build, sell, retain, refinance, repeat — generates equity that strengthens the collateral base for the next round.

I am building a generational enterprise. A responsive, professional Swiss lending partner is exactly the kind of relationship I value.
